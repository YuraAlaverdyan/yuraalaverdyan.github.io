Snoozlings

No. 4Sliding-block puzzle

Shh, the flock is fast asleep. Slide the snoring sheep aside and guide the lost lamb home.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it is

One little lamb is stuck in the middle of the pen. Every pen is a small traffic jam made of wool, and from level 5 you only get so many moves. Think first, slide second.

How to play

  1. Drag a sheep along the line it is lying on
  2. One slide, however far, is one move
  3. Clear a path so the lamb reaches the gate in its colour
  4. Use fewer moves to earn three stars

A look inside

What's in the game

  • Charming 3D sheep that snore, blink, sulk and cheer
  • Sleepy sheep that wake up grumpy, and a sheepdog who never moves a paw
  • The big ram on every tenth level: a boss that slides in every direction
  • Eight worlds to travel through, from Clover Meadow to Lavender Dusk
  • A daily pen with a streak reward, and a clover wheel with a free spin every day
  • 25 things to collect, and an album that shows everything you have found
  • Plays offline, in one hand, a few minutes at a time
