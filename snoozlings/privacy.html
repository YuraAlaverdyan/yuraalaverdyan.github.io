Snoozlings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Snoozlings ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by Pocket Plum Games ("we"). This page says what information is handled when you play it.

What the game stores

The game has no accounts and no server of ours. It never asks for your name, email address or contacts. Your progress (level, coins, hearts, stars, the things you bought in the shop, your settings and the puzzle you are in the middle of) is kept only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • the IP address, from which an approximate location can be worked out;
  • the device model, operating system, language and the version of the game;
  • which ads were shown and how you interacted with them, and diagnostic information.

Google collects this directly. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google describes how it uses such data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow it, the game shows reminders on your device, for example when your hearts are full again. They are scheduled by the game on the device itself; nothing is sent to us. You can switch them off at any time in your device's settings for the game.

Rating the game

The game may open Google Play's own rating window. Whether you rate, and what you write, is between you and Google Play; the game is not told.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). When you first open the game you are asked whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your answer whenever you like with the "Privacy choices" button in the game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID, or switch off ad personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Data on the device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes everything it stored.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given personal information, please write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be put on this page with a new date.
