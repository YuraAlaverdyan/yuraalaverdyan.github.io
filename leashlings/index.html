Leashlings

No. 3Untangle puzzle

The pets went for walkies and got into a terrible tangle. Drag them around until no two leashes cross.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it is

Pups, kitties, bunnies, bears, piglets and chicks are all tied together. It starts easy and gets tricky fast. From level 3 every level has a move limit, so look before you drag.

How to play

  1. Drag a pet to a free spot
  2. Drop a pet on another pet and the two swap places
  3. Red leashes are tangled: free them all to win
  4. Fewer moves, more stars

A look inside

What's in the game

  • Cute 3D pets that smile, worry, cheer and fall asleep
  • Sleepy pets that will not budge, and full-house levels where only swaps work
  • A boss level every ten levels: Big Bruno naps in the middle with everyone tied to him
  • Eight worlds to walk through, from Sunny Park to Candy Land
  • A daily puzzle with a streak reward, a lucky wheel and a piggy bank
  • An album to fill with every friend, world, hat and leash
  • Plays offline, in one hand, in short sessions
