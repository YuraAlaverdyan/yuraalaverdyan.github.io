Jarlings

No. 1Colour-sort puzzle

Jarlings

Round little critters got mixed up in their jars. Tap, drop and match until every jar holds one colour.

  • Endless levels that keep getting trickier
  • Mystery levels, locked jars and giant boss boards
  • Ten hats and four jar styles to collect
Discover Jarlings Coming soon to Google Play

Parcelings

No. 2Fast-swipe arcade

Parcelings

Swipe wide-eyed little parcels into the right delivery van before the conveyor belt speeds up.

  • Quick shifts that get faster with every delivery
  • Gold, heavy and wrapped parcels to keep you guessing
  • Eight depots, each under its own sky
Discover Parcelings Coming soon to Google Play

Leashlings

No. 3Untangle puzzle

Leashlings

The pets went for walkies and got into a terrible tangle. Drag them around until no two leashes cross.

  • Every level can be solved, however knotty it looks
  • Sleepy pets, cushions, puddles and Big Bruno the boss
  • Twelve hats and twelve leash styles
Discover Leashlings Coming soon to Google Play

Snoozlings

No. 4Sliding-block puzzle

Snoozlings

Shh, the flock is fast asleep. Slide the snoring sheep aside and guide the lost lamb home.

  • 500 pens, every one proved solvable
  • Sheepdogs, mud, twin lambs and a big ram boss
  • Dye the wool, hat the lamb, paint the fence
Discover Snoozlings Coming soon to Google Play
