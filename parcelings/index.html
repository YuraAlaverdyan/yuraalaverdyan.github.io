Parcelings

No. 2Fast-swipe arcade

Swipe wide-eyed little parcels into the right delivery van before the conveyor belt speeds up.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it is

The Parcelings ride a conveyor belt, each hoping to land in the right van. Three mistakes and the shift is over, so keep your thumbs quick and your head cool.

How to play

  1. Swipe a parcel left or right to fling it into a van
  2. Match the parcel's colour and shape to the van
  3. Let the sleepy grey duds ride into the bin
  4. Deliver the quota, or last until the clock runs out, to win

A look inside

What's in the game

  • Cute 3D parcels that grin, worry and lean after your finger
  • Vans that swap colours in the middle of a shift
  • Rush hours against the clock, and a 60-second boss shift at the end of every depot
  • A daily challenge with a streak reward, and an endless shift for your personal best
  • Nine outfits and five belt styles to collect
  • Play in English, Russian or Armenian
  • Plays offline, in one hand, in short sessions
