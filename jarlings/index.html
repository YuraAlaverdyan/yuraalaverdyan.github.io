Jarlings

No. 1Colour-sort puzzle

Round little critters got mixed up in their jars. Tap, drop and match until every jar holds one colour.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it is

Meet the Jarlings: round, squishy little critters who got all mixed up in their jars. It starts easy and gets tricky fast. From level 4 every level has a move limit, so think before you tap.

How to play

  1. Tap a jar to pick up the top critter
  2. Tap another jar to drop it in
  3. A critter only goes onto its own colour or into an empty jar
  4. Fill every jar with one colour to win

A look inside

What's in the game

  • Cute 3D critters that blink, cheer, sulk and fall asleep
  • Mystery levels: hidden critters show their colour only when uncovered
  • Tiny jars, home jars and challenge levels with double coins
  • Locked jars, boss levels with giant boards, and new islands as you travel
  • A daily challenge, a lucky wheel and a piggy bank that fills as you win
  • An album to fill with every Jarling, hat and jar
  • Plays offline, in one hand, in short sessions
