Jarlings – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Jarlings ("the game") on Android or iOS. The game is published by Pocket Plum Games ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any account, and we do not run servers that receive information about you. Your game progress (level, coins, lives, hats and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which indicates your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information.

This information is collected by Google directly; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google explains how it uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in its Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). When you first open the game you are asked whether you agree to the use of your data for personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy choices" link on the game's main menu.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Local data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes everything stored on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
